# Service Introduction

### 🌃 Motivation

> How can we help young people in regional communities learn about economics effectively?

- **Bridging Regional Educational Gaps**: Moving beyond economic education concentrated in the Seoul metropolitan area, we help young people in other regions access economic knowledge and apply it to everyday life.
- **Learning Through Practical Experience**: Through AI-based simulated stock trading and financial quizzes, the service goes beyond theoretical instruction, allowing users to experience market fluctuations and investing.
- **Strengthening Future Financial Independence**: Through structured economic education from an early age, the service supports the development of sound financial habits and long-term personal financial management skills.

<br>

### Target Users

- **Young People in Regional Communities**: Providing tailored educational content for young people who have limited access to economic education due to gaps in local educational infrastructure.
- **Parents Interested in Their Children’s Economic Education**: Targeting parents who want to actively participate in and support their children’s economic education and future financial independence.

<br>

### 💎 Service Features

#### 1. Simulated Stock Trading

- Ten local specialty products from the Jeolla region are modeled as fictional publicly traded companies. Their stock prices are affected by temperature, precipitation, and harvest yields.
- Users can buy and sell stocks with an initial capital of KRW 10,000,000, and their jellyfish characters grow according to their investment returns.

#### 2. Jellyfish

- Users earn experience points based on their simulated stock investment returns or by completing economic quizzes, with experience points awarded according to the quiz level.
- The jellyfish’s appearance, including its size and color, changes whenever its experience points reach a certain threshold.

<br>

### 🔋 Expected Benefits

- Provide engaging economic education for young people in regional communities with fewer educational opportunities than those in the Seoul metropolitan area.
- Increase young people’s interest in local agricultural and fishery products, including temperature, precipitation, harvest yields, and seasonal availability.
- Contribute to social initiatives through partnerships with local banks’ everyday financial platforms and regional online agricultural and fishery marketplaces. (Scalability)

# 🛠️ Implementation Process

### AI

- We built an AI model based on XGBoost, considering short-term stock price volatility and its ability to make predictions using multiple variables.
- For precipitation data with many missing values, linear interpolation was used for the data to increase young people’s interest in local agricultural and fishery products, including temperature, precipitation, harvest yields, and seasonal availability.
- To reflect real-time fluctuations, prices were adjusted within a ±2% error range and rounded in Korean won.

<br>

### Data

| Dataset | Coverage | Source | Link |

<br>

### Infra

- The backend and AI servers were run as background processes within a single Amazon EC2 instance.
- Nginx was used for load balancing and HTTPS.
- The frontend GitHub repository was connected to Vercel for rapid deployment.
